Common questions

My drives aren't being recorded

In iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → MilesMGR, choose Always and turn on Precise Location. Also check that Low Power Mode is off, that tracking isn't paused on the Dashboard, and, if you use Work Hours, that the drive was inside them. You can always start a trip by hand from the Dashboard.

One drive was split into two trips, or two drives were joined

Open the trip and use Split trip at a stop, or select trips in the Trips list and tap Merge. Settings → Trip Tracking controls how long a stop has to be before a new trip starts.

A trip is marked red

Red means the trip is missing something the IRS expects, and the line under it says what. Missing addresses are looked up again the next time you open the app. Business trips need a business purpose of a few words, such as "Site visit, Acme Co."

What does "Commute" mean?

Driving between home and your regular workplace. The IRS treats it as personal, so it isn't deductible. Trips from one work location to another, or to a client, are business.

I'm an employee. Why don't I see a tax deduction?

Employees can't deduct unreimbursed mileage on a federal return. Choose Employee in Settings → Deduction or Reimbursement, enter your employer's rate, and MilesMGR shows what you're owed. Reports makes a monthly reimbursement request to hand in.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

MilesMGR backs up once a day to its folder in iCloud Drive, or to a folder you choose, and keeps the last 7. On your new iPhone, install MilesMGR and use Settings → Backup & Restore → Restore from backup. You can also tap Back up now at any time.

How do I get my records to my tax preparer?

Go to More → Reports, pick the year, and share the Mileage log PDF and, if you have them, the Expense report PDF. CSV files are there too for spreadsheets.

The IRS announced a new mileage rate

Rates are updated with the app. Until then, add the official rate in Settings → Mileage Rates and it is used right away.

Restore a purchase or ask for a refund

Use Restore purchase in Settings with the same Apple ID you bought with. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Not tax advice

MilesMGR helps you keep records. It isn't a tax advisor. For decisions about your return, check with a tax professional.
